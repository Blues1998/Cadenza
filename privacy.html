Sharps&Flats

Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

The short version: the Sharps&Flats Android app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your songs, progress and settings stay on your phone. The microphone is used live to hear your notes and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

Who we are

Sharps&Flats is an app for learning songs on guitar and ukulele, made by an independent developer. This policy covers the Android app and the website at blues1998.github.io/sharps-and-flats.

The Android app

What stays on your phone. The songs and chord charts you add, your practice history, scores, the tabs you import and your settings are saved in the app's own storage on your device. We cannot see them. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Microphone. The tuner, voice range and note games listen through the microphone to work out which note you are playing or singing. The sound is analysed live on your phone and thrown away straight after. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent off your device. The app only asks for the microphone when you open a feature that needs it, and you can turn the permission off at any time in Android settings.

Network. All sounds, fonts and pictures are built into the app. The app does not send data to us or to anyone else. The only way anything leaves your phone is if you choose to: for example, by exporting your library as a file and sharing it yourself.

No tracking. There are no ads, analytics, crash reporting tools or third-party trackers in the app.

The website

The website runs the same app in your browser and works the same way, with two additions:

  • Optional account. If you choose to sign in to back up your songs, we store your email address, your songs and your practice history with our database provider, Supabase, so they can sync between browsers. We use this only to provide the backup.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log your IP address when you visit, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Deleting your data

  • Android app: uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings, Apps, Sharps&Flats, Storage, Clear data.
  • Website account: open the account page, sign in and choose Delete account. Your account and everything backed up to it are deleted immediately.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The Android app collects no personal information at all.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a change affects what the app does with your data, we will say so in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/Blues1998/sharps-and-flats/issues.
